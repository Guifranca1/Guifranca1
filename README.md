## 📊 Research Interests

### Quantitative Finance & Econophysics
- **Complex Networks Theory** - Applied to portfolio construction and financial markets
- **Transfer Entropy** - Information flow analysis in time series and market dynamics
- **Risk Management** - Portfolio optimization beyond traditional Markowitz approach
- **Market Microstructure** - Statistical analysis of financial volatility and correlations

📄 **Undergraduate Thesis**: *"Construction of Complex Networks via Transfer Entropy for Portfolio Building"*
